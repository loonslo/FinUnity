FinUnity Terms of Use

Updated: 2026-09-29

Service and accounts

FinUnity provides a personal ledger, asset records, and allocation tools. Keep your password and recovery code safe, and do not share your account. Screenshot recognition may be used only for images you are authorized to process. Do not misuse registration, authentication, recognition, or other interfaces. The service may be temporarily unavailable because of quotas, maintenance, or upstream failures.

Reference data and recognition results

Built-in market data currently consists of fixed integration samples and is labeled as reference data. Check manual prices and recognition results yourself; they are not guaranteed to be real-time, complete, or accurate. The service does not execute trades or provide investment advice. Before confirming an import, check the security, currency, quantity, and cost. You can export JSON when you need an independent backup.

Privacy and account closure

When registering, you must read and agree to the Privacy Policy. You can enter your password in Settings and confirm again to delete your account. The Privacy Policy describes data retention, archiving, backups, and deletion.
