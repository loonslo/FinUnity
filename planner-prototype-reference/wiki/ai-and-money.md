# AI and money

A language model is good at reading a whole financial picture at once and bad at doing
arithmetic in prose. Aurelio gives the model the first job and keeps the second for itself:
the figures come from the records, the model chooses within them, and every change it
proposes waits for you to confirm it.

- [Ask AI whether a stock is worth buying](ask-ai-whether-a-stock-is-worth-buying.md): what a
  useful answer has to contain, and why the amount in it is computed, not written.
- [AI portfolio review: an analysis that argues with itself](ai-portfolio-review.md): an
  analyst who sees only the numbers, a confidant who knows you but never sees them, and an
  answer that keeps their disagreement.
