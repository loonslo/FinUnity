# Guides

Aurelio is a self-hosted personal finance app, and its AI advisor is optional. These guides
take you from nothing to a first answer about your own records.

- [Self-hosted personal finance app: run Aurelio on your own computer](self-hosted-personal-finance-app.md)
  covers the three tools it needs, the one command that starts it, the demo household, and
  where your records live.
- [An AI financial advisor with your own OpenRouter key](ai-financial-advisor-with-your-own-openrouter-key.md)
  explains the key, a cap on what it can spend, and how to choose the model that answers.
- [How much does an AI financial advisor cost?](how-much-does-an-ai-financial-advisor-cost.md)
  shows what real requests cost, and what makes one question dearer than another.
