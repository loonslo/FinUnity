# ETFs and funds

A fund is a basket, and two baskets with different names can hold the same things. These
pages are about what is inside your funds and what they do with what they earn.

- [ETF overlap and portfolio X-ray](etf-overlap-and-portfolio-x-ray.md): the companies,
  countries, sectors and currencies inside your funds, added up, and where they overlap.
- [Accumulating vs distributing ETFs](accumulating-vs-distributing-etf.md): one difference,
  what happens to the dividends, and which is better for whom.
