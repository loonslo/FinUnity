# Net worth and cash

Net worth is everything you own minus everything you owe. Aurelio builds every part of it the
same way: a record you entered, with a date, plus the events dated after it, so a wrong
number always traces back to a wrong record or a wrong event.

- [How to calculate net worth](how-to-calculate-net-worth.md): the formula, a worked example
  with a mortgage, and how each figure is built.
- [Personal finance without bank sync](personal-finance-without-bank-sync.md): one real
  balance per account, and the income and expenses that move it.
